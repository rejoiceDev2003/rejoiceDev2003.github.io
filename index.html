Projects I’m proud of

Work I built for Investor Hosting Centre, a digital platform connecting global investors with bankable opportunities across Africa.

Web app

Investor Hosting Centre Web App

The Investor Hosting Centre web platform, where investors explore bankable opportunities in Mining, Agriculture and Real Estate through a live opportunity feed and search, and opportunity owners upload their projects.

Visit the website
Mobile app

Investor Hosting Centre Mobile App

InvestorHosting Operations, the team app for IHC staff. It brings tasks, leads, opportunities, profiles, meetings and dashboards into one place, and lets staff take over chatbot conversations when a customer is waiting for a human.

AI chatbot

IHC AI, Investment Intelligence Assistant

An AI assistant on the Investor Hosting Centre website. It searches the live IHC opportunity register by industry, commodity, ticket size and return, helps owners prepare their opportunities for investors and connects users with specialists. It supports voice input, and its answers are grounded in real platform data.
